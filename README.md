# Forecasting Automotive Sales in Emerging Markets: Tree-Based vs. Linear Extrapolation

[cite_start]This repository contains the complete codebase, datasets, and visualizations for my Master's Research Project in the Department of Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at NUST[cite: 4, 13].

## 📌 Research Question
**Can the integration of exogenous macroeconomic variables (PKR devaluation, SBP interest rates) into linear machine learning models outperform traditional autoregressive tree-based models (XGBoost, Random Forest) when forecasting automotive sales during a Black Swan economic crash?**
